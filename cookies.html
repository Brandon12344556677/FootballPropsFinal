Cookies Policy

Effective 22 September 2026 · Last updated 22 September 2026

The short version: Prop Streak Lab does not use cookies, advertising trackers, or cross-site tracking. To work, it saves a little information in your browser's local storage, which stays on your device. Analytics are cookieless.

What we store in your browser

We use your browser's local storage — not cookies — to remember your preferences between visits. This information never leaves your device and is not transmitted to us:

WhatWhy
Watchlist & comparison listRemember the players you've saved or are comparing.
Recent inputs & view preferencesKeep the tools set the way you left them.
Notice dismissedRemember that you've closed the privacy notice so it doesn't reappear.

You can delete all of this at any time by clearing site data in your browser settings. The site keeps working if you do — it just forgets your preferences.

Analytics (cookieless)

We use GoatCounter to count visits and see which pages are popular. It sets no cookies, uses no persistent identifier, and does not track you across other sites. See our Privacy Policy for what it records.

No advertising or marketing cookies

We do not use advertising, retargeting, or social-media marketing cookies or pixels of any kind.

Third-party content

Some content loads directly in your browser from third parties (Google Fonts, a CDN used only for the bet-slip screenshot tool, and Polymarket for live prices). We don't set cookies through them for our own purposes; any storage those services use is governed by their own policies, listed in our Privacy Policy.

Do you need to consent?

Because we only use essential local storage and cookieless analytics — and no advertising or tracking cookies — a consent gate is not required. We show a short notice for transparency, which you can dismiss.
