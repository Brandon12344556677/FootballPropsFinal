What Prop Streak Lab does

The data behind every prop — not a hot take.

Most prop "advice" is a guess with confidence. We show you the numbers and let you decide.

Every game log

See how often any player has cleared any line — last 5, last 10, all season — with the misses shown right next to the hits.

A calibrated chance

A recency-weighted model turns the log into a probability with an honest range, adjusted for the matchup — and it checks itself against its own track record.

Priced vs. the market

We line the model up against live Polymarket prices so you can see the edge — or see that there isn't one.

Graded in public

Picks are recorded before the game and graded from the box score — wins and losses. Nothing gets deleted.
