⚡ Value spots

overs only: market gives it 30–60%, model is 15+ points better, 20+ games of history
What counts as a value spot — both rules have to be true
  1. 1The market gives it at least a 30% chance. The price you'd pay has to be 30¢ or more. This is the rule that keeps penny longshots off the list — a 6¢ ask against an 11% model chance looks like a huge edge in percentage terms and is still a bad bet.
  2. 2Our model puts it at least 15 points higher. 30¢ ask → needs 45% · 50¢ → 65% · 70¢ → 85%. The gap between the two, in percentage points, is the edge shown in the Polymarket column.
Both rules are tested against the cheaper of the Polymarket and Kalshi prices for the side the model takes that has at least $25 offered at it (or within 2¢) — the price you'd actually pay; a thinner price would move when you bet — and a prop also needs 6+ effective games of history behind it. Everything clearing both rules is listed, ranked by that edge. Prices come from Polymarket's NBA player-prop markets, so this list stays empty until those are posted. Props waiting on injury news stay off this list and Top 25 Surest: when the player, or a regular teammate at his position (guards, forwards, centers), is day-to-day on ESPN's injury report — until he's ruled out (then his teammates' props come back) or cleared.
