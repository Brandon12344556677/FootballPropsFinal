What counts as a value spot — both rules have to be true
  1. 1Polymarket gives it at least a 35% chance. The price you'd pay has to be 35¢ or more. This is the rule that keeps penny longshots off the list — a 6¢ ask against an 11% model chance looks like a huge edge in percentage terms and is still a bad bet.
  2. 2Our model puts it at least 25 points higher. 35¢ ask → needs 60% · 50¢ → 75% · 70¢ → 95%. The gap between the two, in percentage points, is the edge shown in the Polymarket column.
Both rules are tested against the Polymarket price for the side the model takes — the price you'd actually pay — and a prop also needs 6+ effective games of history behind it. The 20 with the highest model chance that clear both rules are listed, ranked by model chance. Prices come from Polymarket's NHL player-prop markets, so this list stays empty until those are posted.
