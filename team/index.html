                  Team only

                                        Before you run them

                                        • Meta treats real-money gambling ads as restricted. Keep ads about research and the public record: no "bet", "lock", "free money" or exchange names, and say "18+" on the ad (every ad here does).
                                        • Target 18+ only (the site's age check), and decide which states to leave out before the first campaign.
                                        • Hit rates need their price. Top 25 picks hit often because they're priced 85¢+. Keep the units line on record ads, and never write "guaranteed".
                                        • The landing page shows the Kalshi / Polymarket sign-up pills. Meta's review may count those as gambling promotion. Consider hiding them for visitors arriving from Facebook.
                                        • Video ads follow the same rules: the script avoids those words, and the end card carries the 18+ line. Read the script as written, or keep any changes to research and the record.
                                        • Start small: 3–4 ad kinds, 2–3 versions each, about $20–30 a day per ad set. After 5 days, cut the weakest and make more of the best.
