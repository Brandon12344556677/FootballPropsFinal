Privacy Policy

Effective 22 September 2026 · Last updated 22 September 2026

The short version: Prop Streak Lab has no accounts and no logins. We don't sell your data, run ads, or build advertising profiles. Almost everything the site remembers stays inside your own browser, where we can't read it. The only information that ever reaches us is a message you deliberately email us.

Who we are

Prop Streak Lab (propstreaklab.com) is an independent sports-data project operated by an individual (a sole proprietor). You can reach us at info@propstreaklab.com. This policy explains what happens to information when you use the site.

Information you give us

The site has no sign-up and no data-collecting forms. The one exception is the “Suggest a feature” box. When you use it, the site opens your own email app with a draft addressed to us — nothing is sent automatically. If you choose to send that email, we receive your email address and your message, and we keep it only as long as needed to read and respond. The player search, line inputs, parlay builder and other controls run entirely in your browser and are never transmitted to us.

The bet-slip screenshot tool

If you drop or paste a screenshot into the bet-slip reader, the image is processed locally in your browser (using an on-device text-recognition library). The image is never uploaded to us or to anyone else, and we never see it.

Stored in your browser (not sent to us)

To make the site usable, we save small pieces of information in your browser's local storage — for example your watchlist, comparison list, recent inputs, and whether you've dismissed the privacy notice. This data stays on your device, is not a cookie, and is not transmitted to us. You can clear it any time from your browser settings. See our Cookies Policy for details.

Analytics

We use GoatCounter, a privacy-friendly, cookieless analytics tool, to understand roughly how many people visit and which pages are popular. It does not set cookies, does not track you across other websites, and does not build a personal profile. It records aggregate information such as page views, the referring site, a rough country, and browser type. See GoatCounter's privacy note.

Third-party services that may see your IP address

Because parts of the site load content directly in your browser, a few third parties may receive your IP address (as any website visit involves). We don't control their processing; each has its own policy.

  • GitHub Pages hosts the site and may process standard server-log data (IP, browser) as our host. See GitHub's privacy statement.
  • Google Fonts serves the site's typefaces; Google may log your IP when a font loads. See Google's policy.
  • jsDelivr (CDN) is used only if you open the bet-slip screenshot reader, to load the on-device text-recognition library.
  • Polymarket — live prices shown on the site are fetched from Polymarket in your browser, so your IP is visible to Polymarket. Some links to Polymarket are affiliate/referral links (see our Terms). See Polymarket's policy.

How we use information

We use the email you send only to handle your request, and aggregate analytics only to understand and improve the site. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Your choices and rights

Because we collect so little, there is usually nothing on our side to access or delete beyond an email you've sent us. Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal information (for example under the GDPR or CCPA/CPRA). To make a request, email info@propstreaklab.com. You can clear all locally-stored data yourself through your browser settings at any time.

Children

The site is general-audience and informational. It is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

International visitors

The site is hosted in the United States. By using it, you understand that information involved in your visit is processed in the United States and other countries where our providers operate.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time; the “Last updated” date above will change accordingly. Material changes will be reflected on this page.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email info@propstreaklab.com.
