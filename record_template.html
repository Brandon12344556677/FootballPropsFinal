Model vs. market

Brier score: the average squared gap between a stated chance and what happened (1 for a hit, 0 for a miss). Lower is better; always saying 50% scores 0.250. Picks are the graded ones that had a price. Weight is the share of the model that scored best on that row's picks, refit on every update, never under 10% or over 90%. The cards in every sport use the top row's weight; the rows under it show what each sport would get on its own. How it's worked out.

How this page counts

Only picks recorded before their game count: no backtests. "Model" is the average chance the model gave those picks, to set beside how often they hit. A pick is a hit or a miss; pushes, voids and players who didn't play are left out. Units are 1 unit on each pick at its recorded Polymarket or Kalshi price: a hit at 40¢ returns +1.50, a miss returns −1. A pick with no price, or a price that can't have been real (under 10¢, or 35¢ away from its first price), counts in the hit rate but not in the units. Return is units divided by priced picks. Price move is how the price of the side taken changed from its first recording to its last before the game. The MLB Value list has been unders only since its overs lost; those overs keep their own row here. Past results don't predict future ones. Research and entertainment, not advice. 18+.
